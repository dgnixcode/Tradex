Status: 2026-09-03 | every risk identified across the 22 research documents, sorted by expected severity. Severity is loss magnitude × likelihood × how long it stays undetected. The last column is the honest residual after the stated mitigation.

## The five that should keep the owner awake

Chosen for expected loss, not for how alarming they sound.

| Risk | Why this one | The cheapest thing that materially reduces it |
|---|---|---|
| **R07** - per-IP rate limit | It silently decides whether the platform serves 400 accounts or 40,000, it is being guessed at, and since R04 was retired it is the **only** remaining gate inside the plan | **The two-hour experiment.** Two keys, one IP, read-only calls. There is no reason to defer it |
| **R01** - key exfiltration | Public precedent, exact same configuration, no exchange-side control available, and the loss is every customer's balance | Ship the **separate signer process** at the first real-money phase rather than "later", and the decrypt-rate alert with it. Cost: a few days |
| **R08** - PMLA registration | 53 providers have already had apps and URLs taken down. The activity definition reads directly onto storing API keys | Build **KYC-capable and 5-year-retention** in Phase 00 - a schema decision, not a feature - then get counsel's answer before launch |
| **R06** - reconciler silence | It is the only risk where the system is *fully broken while appearing healthy*, and it is the cheapest to eliminate | **Alert `A3`**: page on the absence of reconciliation progress, plus the rule that an unknown status alarms instead of throwing. Cost: an afternoon |
| **R28** - no demand | No India-specific competitor surfaced. It is the only risk engineering cannot touch, and the only one where the right answer might be to stop | **Five customer conversations** before Phase 02 |

R04 previously headed this list. It was retired on 2026-09-05 by a scope decision rather than a mitigation: v1 displays no CoinDCX-derived prices, so the clause has nothing to reach (`ARCHITECTURE` §6a). Three of the five remaining items still cost less than a week of work between them.
